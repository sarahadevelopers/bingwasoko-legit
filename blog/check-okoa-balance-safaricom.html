📌 What is Okoa Jahazi?

Okoa Jahazi is Safaricom's emergency credit service that allows you to borrow airtime when your balance reaches zero. You can borrow Ksh 10, 20, 50, or 100 depending on your usage history and eligibility. The borrowed amount is automatically deducted from your next airtime top‑up. While convenient, it is important to keep track of your outstanding balance to avoid accumulating debt that may block future purchases.

📌 Quick fact: Okoa Jahazi loans must be repaid within 7 days. If you don't top up within that period, your line may be restricted from making outgoing calls or using data until you clear the debt.

📱 Method 1: Check Okoa Balance via USSD (*100#)

The quickest way to check your Okoa Jahazi balance is by using the Safaricom USSD menu. Follow these steps:

  1. Dial *100# on your Safaricom line.
  2. Select My Account (usually option 1).
  3. Choose Okoa Jahazi or Check Balance (the menu may vary).
  4. Your outstanding Okoa loan amount and repayment status will be displayed on the screen.

You will also receive an SMS confirming your balance and the repayment deadline.

⚠️ Note: If you have not borrowed any Okoa credit, you will see a message stating that you have no outstanding loan.

📲 Method 2: Check via MySafaricom App

For a more detailed view of your Okoa Jahazi history and repayment schedule, use the MySafaricom app.

  1. Open the MySafaricom app and log in.
  2. Tap on the Account tab (bottom menu).
  3. Scroll down to the Okoa Jahazi section.
  4. You will see your outstanding balance, the date you borrowed, and the amount due.

The app also allows you to set reminders for repayment and view your transaction history.

📬 Method 3: Other Ways to Check Okoa Balance

  • SMS: You can send a blank SMS to 100 (Safaricom's shortcode) – you will receive an automated reply with your account summary, including Okoa balance.
  • Customer Care: Call 100 and follow the voice prompts to check your Okoa status, or speak to a customer service agent.
  • USSD (*544#): Some users can check Okoa balance by dialling *544# and navigating to “My Account” or “Okoa Jahazi”.

📡 Checking Airtel Okoa (Pewa) Balance

Airtel Kenya offers a similar service called Pewa or Airtel Okoa. To check your Airtel Okoa balance:

  • Dial *544# → Select My Account → Airtel Okoa.
  • Alternatively, use the MyAirtel app – go to Account → Okoa.
  • For a full guide, read our Airtel Okoa (Pewa) guide.
