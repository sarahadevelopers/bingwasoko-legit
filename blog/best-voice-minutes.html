📞 Can Safaricom Minutes Call Airtel?

Yes – minutes from both Safaricom and Airtel can call any network in Kenya. However:

  • On-net calls (Safaricom to Safaricom, Airtel to Airtel) are usually cheaper
  • Off-net calls (Safaricom to Airtel, vice versa) may cost more or use minutes differently
  • Always check your bundle's terms – some minutes are "all-network" while others are "on-net only"
⚠️ Important: Always check if your minutes bundle covers all networks or only the same network. On-net-only bundles won't work for cross-network calls.

💳 Airtime vs Voice Minutes – What's the Difference?

  • Airtime is the general credit on your phone – can be used for calls, SMS, and data
  • Voice minutes are specific bundles that only cover calls (and sometimes only to the same network)
  • Voice minutes are usually cheaper per minute than using regular airtime

❓ What is Tubonge on Safaricom?

Tubonge is NOT a Safaricom product – it is Airtel's voice minutes product. If you're looking for Safaricom voice bundles, check Nikoze or standard combo bundles.

❓ Can Safaricom minutes call Airtel?

Yes – minutes from both Safaricom and Airtel can call any network. However, on-net calls (same network) are usually cheaper than off-net calls (different network).

Airtime is general credit for calls, SMS, and data. Voice minutes are specific bundles that only cover calls – they're usually cheaper per minute than using regular airtime.
