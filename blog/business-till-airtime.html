📱 What is a Till Number?

A till number (also called a "paybill" or "merchant code" in some contexts) is a unique identifier used by businesses and resellers to receive payments via M-PESA. When you pay to a till number via Buy Goods and Services, you can purchase airtime, bundles, minutes, and data instantly.

📌 Key difference: Unlike a Paybill (which requires an account number), a till number only requires you to enter the amount – making it faster and simpler.

📞 How to Buy Airtime Using Till Number

  1. Open M-PESA on your phone
  2. Select Lipa na M-PESA
