📱 What is Airtel Data Sharing?

Airtel data sharing allows users to purchase data bundles and gift them to another Airtel number or share usage across multiple devices. This feature is useful for:

  • Gifting data: Send bundles to friends or family
  • Multiple devices: One bundle powers phones, MiFi, and tablets
  • Emergency data: Help someone who runs out of bundles
  • Family plans: Parents sharing data with children
📌 Key difference: Airtel calls it "Gift a Bundle" or "Share Data", while Safaricom calls it "Sambaza". They are similar services on different networks.

📞 Method 1: Share Airtel Data via USSD (*544#)

To gift or share Airtel data bundles using USSD:

  1. Dial *544# from your Airtel line
  2. Select "Bundles" or "Data"
  3. Choose "Gift a Bundle" or "Share Data"
  4. Enter the recipient's Airtel phone number
  5. Select the bundle you want to gift
  6. Confirm – the bundle is sent instantly
⚠️ Important: Not all Airtel bundles are shareable. Only specific packages allow gifting. Check the bundle terms before purchasing. If the "Gift a Bundle" option isn't available, the bundle may not be shareable.
