Tips to Reduce Data Usage and Save More

  • Use Wi‑Fi whenever available – at home, work, or public hotspots.
  • Limit video streaming resolution – watch YouTube at 360p instead of 1080p.
  • Enable data saver mode – in Chrome, WhatsApp, Instagram, and other apps.
  • Download offline content – maps, playlists, and videos ahead of time.
  • Restrict background data – prevent apps from syncing when not in use.
  • Use lite versions of apps – Facebook Lite, YouTube Go, etc., consume far less data.
