Election days in Kenya often come with network congestion, USSD failures, and occasional service disruptions. To stay connected – whether for news, family communication, or work – it is essential to prepare in advance. This guide explains how to buy cheap bundles beforehand, avoid Okoa Jahazi problems, and ensure you have data and airtime even when networks are under heavy load.

🗳️ Why You Need to Prepare in Advance

  • USSD menus may fail: *544# and other codes often become slow or unresponsive during peak hours.
  • M‑PESA congestion: STK pushes may be delayed, but once initiated, they usually go through.
  • Okoa restrictions still apply: Official channels will reject purchases if you have outstanding airtime debt.
  • Reseller tills are more reliable: Lipa na M‑PESA Buy Goods tends to work even when USSD is down.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Election Day Bundles

What if I buy a bundle now but election day is tomorrow – will it expire?
Check the validity of the bundle. For example, a 'Midnight' bundle is only usable between 11 PM and 6 AM. If you buy it in the morning, it will not work until that night. For all‑day coverage, choose '24 Hours' or '7 Days' bundles.
Can I use a reseller till if I have Okoa debt?
Yes – reseller tills like 1621614 bypass Okoa checks. The bundle will be delivered even if you owe airtime.
What happens if M‑PESA itself is slow?
STK push may take a few minutes, but once you confirm the PIN, the transaction is queued. The bundle will arrive as soon as the network processes it. Avoid sending multiple requests.
Can I buy bundles for my children's phones in advance?
Yes – use the 'Gift a Friend' feature on the reseller's website. You can buy the bundle and have it sent directly to their number. They will have connectivity without needing to do anything.
Why do USSD bundles fail on election day?
During high traffic, USSD gateways become congested. Also, if you have Okoa debt, official channels will reject your purchase. Reseller tills avoid both issues.
How far in advance should I buy election day bundles?
Purchase at least 2–3 days before election day. This ensures you beat the rush and have time to resolve any transaction issues.
