Affordable Pricing

Reliable Coverage

Ways Students Can Save Data

Managing internet usage carefully can help students stretch their bundles further.

  • Download notes using Wi-Fi when possible
  • Reduce video streaming quality
  • Disable background app updates
  • Use night data for large downloads
  • Monitor daily usage regularly
