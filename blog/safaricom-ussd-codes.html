Checking your airtime balance is one of the most common Safaricom actions.

  2. Dial *144#
  3. Press call

📌 Tip: Save *144# as a contact for quick access. It works on all phones without internet.

To check your remaining data, minutes, or SMS balance:

🔄 Okoa Jahazi Complete Guide (*131#)

Okoa Jahazi is Safaricom's emergency airtime loan service. Dial *131# to access it. Below is everything you need to know.

📞 How to Borrow Okoa Jahazi

  1. Dial *131# from your Safaricom line
  2. Select "Okoa Jahazi" (or "Okoa Chap Chap" depending on your menu)
  3. Choose the amount you want to borrow (options vary by eligibility)
  4. Confirm the loan – you'll see the amount plus service fee
  5. You receive a confirmation SMS and the airtime is added instantly

📊 How to Check Your Okoa Balance

  1. Dial *131#
  2. Select "Okoa Jahazi"
  3. Choose "Check Balance" or "Okoa Balance"
  4. Your outstanding loan amount and available borrowing limit are displayed

Alternative: You can also check your Okoa balance by dialling *131*3# (varies by menu version).

💰 How to Repay Okoa Jahazi

  • Auto-deduction: Your Okoa loan is automatically deducted when you top up airtime
  • Early repayment: Dial *131# → "Okoa Jahazi" → "Repay Okoa" → Follow the prompts

⭐ Bonga Points Code (*126#)

Check and redeem your Bonga Points using USSD.

  1. Dial *126#

  3. Choose "Redeem" to convert points to airtime or data bundles
  4. Confirm your selection – the value is added instantly

📲 How to Sambaza Airtime (*140#)

Transfer airtime to another Safaricom number using Sambaza.

USSD Format:

*140*Amount*PhoneNumber#

Example: *140*50*0712345678# sends Ksh 19 airtime.

  1. Dial the formatted USSD code
  2. Confirm the transaction
  3. The recipient receives the airtime instantly
📌 Note: A small transaction fee applies. You must have sufficient airtime balance to send.

📞 Please Call Me Code (*130#)

Send a "Please Call Me" request to another Safaricom number when you're out of airtime.

  1. Dial *130#
  2. Enter the recipient's Safaricom phone number
  3. Confirm – the recipient receives a free SMS requesting a call back

Alternative format: *130*PhoneNumber# (e.g., *130*0712345678#).
