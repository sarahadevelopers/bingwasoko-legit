What is Fuliza?

Fuliza is an M-PESA overdraft service that lets eligible users complete transactions even when their balance is insufficient.

Instead of a failed transaction, Safaricom temporarily covers the amount and you repay later.

Can You Buy Airtime Using Fuliza?

Yes. Many users successfully purchase bundles and airtime through M-PESA while using Fuliza balances.

This is especially useful during emergencies, late-night browsing, online classes, work meetings, or urgent communication needs.

Important:

Your Fuliza limit depends on your Safaricom usage history and account activity.
