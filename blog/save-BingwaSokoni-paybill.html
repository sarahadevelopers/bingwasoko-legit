Why Save Till 1621614?

  • Speed: Buy bundles in under 10 seconds – no need to type the till number each time.
  • Accuracy: Avoid sending money to the wrong till due to a typo.
  • Convenience: Access your favourite payment methods from the M‑PESA main menu.
  • Still works with Okoa: Saved till numbers do not affect the Okoa‑bypass feature.

Method 1 – Save via SIM Toolkit (USSD)

If you use the classic M‑PESA SIM toolkit (the *334# menu), follow these steps:

  1. Dial *334# and press send.
  2. Select My Account (usually option 7).
  3. Select Favourites (option 2).
  4. Choose Add New Favourite.
  5. Select Buy Goods and Services (or Lipa na M‑PESA).
  6. Enter the till number: 1621614.
  7. Optionally, give it a nickname like “Bingwa Bundles”.
  8. Confirm – the till is saved.

Next time you want to buy bundles, go to M‑PESA → Favourites → select Bingwa Bundles → enter amount → PIN.

⚠️ Note: The exact menu options may vary slightly depending on your SIM card and phone. Look for “Favourites” or “My Favourites” under the My Account section.

Method 2 – Save via My M‑PESA App (Smartphone)

If you use the My M‑PESA app, saving a favourite till is even easier:

  1. Open the My M‑PESA app and log in.
  2. Tap Lipa na M‑PESA → Buy Goods and Services.
  3. Enter Till Number 162 161 4 .
  4. Before confirming the payment, tap the Save as favourite option (star icon).
  5. Give it a name (e.g., “Bingwa Till”).
  6. Complete the transaction or cancel – the till will still be saved.

You can now find it under Favourites on the app’s home screen.

How to Use the Saved Till to Buy Bundles

  1. Open M‑PESA → Favourites → Select “Bingwa Till”.
  2. Enter the exact amount for your chosen bundle (e.g., 55 for 1 GB Tunukiwa).
  3. Confirm with your PIN – the bundle is delivered instantly.

No need to remember or type the till number again.

Common Issues & Troubleshooting

  • “Favourites” option not visible: Some older SIM cards may not support favourites. Try updating your SIM at a Safaricom shop, or use the M‑PESA app instead.
  • Saved till not appearing: Ensure you saved it correctly. You can also re‑add it.
  • Transaction fails: Check that you are sending the exact amount. The saved till does not change the required amount.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Saving Till 1621614

❓ Can I save multiple tills?

Yes – you can save up to 5 favourites on most SIM toolkits, and many more in the M‑PESA app.

❓ Will saving the till affect my Okoa debt?

No – saving the till does not change how the transaction works. You still get the bundle even with Okoa debt.

❓ I saved the till but now I want to remove it – how?

Go to Favourites → Manage Favourites → Delete → Select the till → Confirm.

❓ Does the saved till work for both Safaricom and Airtel bundles?

Yes – the till number is the same. The bundle type depends on the amount you send. Check the price list before sending.
