What Is Safaricom Tunukiwa?

Tunukiwa is Safaricom’s personalized offer system that gives selected customers discounted bundles, extra data and special voice offers.

  • Offers vary between users
  • Some customers receive discounted data
  • Others receive bonus minutes or SMS
  • Offers change frequently

Safaricom describes Tunukiwa as a personalized customer offer platform that provides special bundle deals based on customer usage patterns.

What Are Airtel Amazing Bundles?

Airtel Amazing bundles are affordable prepaid internet and combo offers available to many Airtel Kenya users.

  • Affordable daily bundles
  • Weekly and monthly internet packages
  • Combo offers with minutes and SMS
  • Often lower pricing compared to competitors

Airtel Kenya promotes Amazing Data Bundles as low-cost internet packages aimed at helping users access affordable connectivity.

Which One Is Better for Heavy Data Users?

Heavy internet users usually compare total value per gigabyte.

Many Kenyan users prefer Airtel for cheaper large-volume browsing while relying on Safaricom for stronger coverage stability.
