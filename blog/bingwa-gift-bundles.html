📱 Step‑by‑Step Guide to Buying Bundles for Another Number

  1. Visit the Bingwa Sokoni website – Go to the homepage or bundles section.
  2. Select your bundle – Choose from data, minutes, SMS, or combo packages. Check the price (e.g., 1GB 1‑hour = Ksh 19).
  3. Choose "Buy for Another Number" – On the checkout page, look for the option labelled "Gift a Friend" or "Buy for Someone Else".
  4. Enter the recipient's phone number – Type the Safaricom or Airtel number you want to send bundles to. Double‑check the digits!
  5. Pay via M‑PESA – You will be given the Till Number 162 161 4 and the exact amount to pay.
  6. Complete the M‑PESA transaction – Open M‑PESA → Lipa na M‑PESA → Buy Goods and Services → Enter Till 1621614 → Enter the exact amount → Enter your PIN → Confirm.
  7. Wait for confirmation – Within seconds, both you and the recipient will receive SMS confirmations. The bundle is now active on the recipient's line.

That is it – no USSD menus, no long codes, and no need for the recipient to do anything.

⚠️ Critical: Always confirm the recipient's phone number before completing the M‑PESA payment. If you send to the wrong number, you may lose the bundle. Support can help, but it is easier to prevent mistakes upfront.
