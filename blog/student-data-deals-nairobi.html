Maximizing Your Data as a Student

  • Use Wi‑Fi on campus – many universities offer free or low‑cost Wi‑Fi in libraries and common areas.
  • Download lectures during off‑peak hours – use the Midnight bundle (11 PM – 6 AM) for large downloads.
  • Compress data usage – enable data saver in Chrome, use Lite versions of apps, and limit video quality to 360p.
  • Share bundles with friends – some reseller platforms allow gifting, so you can split costs.
  • Avoid auto‑renewal – official bundles may renew automatically; reseller tills never do.
