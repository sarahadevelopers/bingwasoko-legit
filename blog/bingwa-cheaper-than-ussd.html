💡 Why Are Reseller Bundles Cheaper?

Resellers buy bundles in bulk at wholesale rates or leverage promotional dealer lines. They then resell individual bundles to customers at a markup that is still lower than the official retail price. This is similar to how a shop buys goods in bulk and sells cheaper than the manufacturer’s suggested price.

  • Bulk purchasing – resellers buy thousands of bundles at discounted rates.
  • No carrier overhead – they don’t pay for retail infrastructure, USSD development, or advertising.
  • Promotional offers – sometimes they use targeted “Tunukiwa” or “Amazing” deals that are not available to the general public through USSD.
  • Automated systems – low operational costs allow them to pass savings to customers.
