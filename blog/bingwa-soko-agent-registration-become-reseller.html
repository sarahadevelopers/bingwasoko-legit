Tips for Successful Reselling

📌 Best Practices

  1. Know Your Products: Understand the bundles you're selling – data, minutes, SMS, prices, and validity.
  2. Build a Customer Base: Start with friends, family, and colleagues. Ask them to refer others.
  3. Use WhatsApp Groups: Create groups where you can share bundle deals and special offers.
  4. Be Responsive: Reply quickly to customer inquiries. Fast service builds trust.
  5. Share Promotions: Regularly share special offers and discounts with your customers.
  6. Track Your Sales: Keep a record of your sales to identify what bundles are most popular.
  7. Stay Consistent: Regular communication with your customers keeps you top of mind.
