❌ Why Official Purchases Fail with Okoa Debt

Safaricom built its credit recovery system to prioritise loan repayment. When you have an outstanding Okoa balance:

  • USSD purchases (*544#) – will either fail with an error message or redirect your payment to repay the loan.
  • M‑PESA buy airtime – also goes through the same system and detects your debt.

This is because USSD airtime and minutes purchases are classified as "network top‑ups" – they are directly linked to the loan repayment system. The network assumes you are adding value, and it deducts the loan first.

⚠️ Common error: "You have an outstanding Okoa Jahazi. Please clear it before purchasing." This blocks most official channels.
