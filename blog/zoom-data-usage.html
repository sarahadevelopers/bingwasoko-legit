📊 Zoom Data Consumption per Hour (Estimates)

Zoom’s data usage depends on video quality, whether you are using audio only, and if screen sharing is active.

Call Type / QualityData per Hour (approx.)
Audio only (no video) 10–30 MB
Low quality video (240p / 320p) 100–200 MB
Standard quality (360p–480p) 300–500 MB
HD quality (720p) 600–900 MB
Full HD (1080p) – with active speaker 1.2–1.5 GB
Screen sharing + video Adds ~50–100 MB/hour

One hour of standard Zoom meeting (360p) will use about 400 MB. A full workday of 4–5 hours could consume 2 GB or more.

⚠️ Important: If you have limited data, turn off your video when not needed. Audio‑only calls use a fraction of the data.

💡 Tips to Reduce Zoom Data Usage

  • Turn off video when you are not speaking. This is the biggest data saver.
  • Lower video quality. In Zoom settings, uncheck “Enable HD”. Stick to standard definition (360p).
  • Use audio‑only mode when possible. Join meetings with video off and only turn it on when necessary.
  • Limit screen sharing. Sharing your screen adds extra data consumption. Share only when required.
  • Close other apps. Background apps may consume bandwidth and affect call quality.
  • Use a wired connection if possible. Ethernet is more stable, reducing retransmissions.
  • Schedule shorter meetings. Every minute counts.
