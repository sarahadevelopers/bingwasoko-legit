What Are Safaricom Tunukiwa Offers?

Tunukiwa (Swahili for “get rewarded”) are time‑limited promotions that give you extra data, minutes, or SMS at a reduced price. They appear as pop‑ups in the MySafaricom app or as targeted SMS offers. Examples include:

  • “Get 2GB for the price of 1GB”
  • “50% off your next daily bundle”
  • “Free 100 SMS when you buy a weekly plan”

These offers are usually valid for a few days and can be redeemed via USSD or the app.

⚠️ Note: Tunukiwa offers are not available to everyone at the same time. Safaricom targets specific subscribers based on usage history. You may see an offer while your friend does not.

How to Check for Tunukiwa Offers

  1. MySafaricom App: Open the app and look for the “Tunukiwa” banner or “Offers” section.
  2. SMS notifications: Safaricom sends SMS alerts with promo codes or USSD instructions.
  3. Dial *544#: Sometimes a Tunukiwa option appears on the main menu.

If you do not see any offer, you can try buying a regular bundle – the system may occasionally prompt a “special offer” after purchase.
