Detailed Steps:

  1. Select Your Bundle: Browse and choose your preferred bundle on bingwasoko.co.ke.
  2. Click "Buy Now": This will show you the exact amount to pay.
  3. Open M-PESA: On your phone, go to the M-PESA menu.
  4. Select "Lipa na M-PESA": Choose this option from the menu.
  5. Choose "Buy Goods & Services": This is where you'll enter the till number.
  6. Enter Till Number: Enter 1621614 as the till number.
  7. Enter the Amount: Enter the exact amount for your chosen bundle.
  8. Confirm with Your PIN: Enter your M-PESA PIN and confirm the payment.
  9. Receive Your Bundle: Your bundle is delivered within seconds.
⚠️ Important: Always enter the exact amount shown on the website. Paying the wrong amount may delay your delivery.

What Happens After Payment?

  • Instant Confirmation: You'll receive an M-PESA confirmation message.
  • Bundle Delivery: Your bundle is sent to your number within seconds.
  • Delivery SMS: You'll receive an SMS from the network confirming your bundle activation.
  • Use Your Bundle: You can start using your data, minutes, or SMS immediately.
⏱️ Typical Delivery Time: 5-15 seconds after payment confirmation. In rare cases, delivery may take up to 2 minutes.
