📱 What is STK? (Sim Toolkit)

STK stands for Sim Toolkit. It's a technology built into your SIM card that allows your mobile network operator (like Safaricom) to send interactive menus and prompts directly to your phone.

📌 STK Meaning: Sim Toolkit = a set of applications on your SIM card that enable mobile payments, USSD services, and other network interactions.

📲 What is STK Push in M-PESA?

STK Push is a Safaricom M-PESA payment method where a payment prompt is automatically sent directly to your phone. Instead of manually entering Paybill or Till details every time, the payment request comes to your phone as a popup.

  • You enter your phone number on a website or app
  • A payment popup appears on your phone instantly
  • You enter your M-PESA PIN
  • The payment is completed automatically

This is how Bingwa Sokoni delivers bundles instantly – when you pay via Till 1621614, the STK Push prompt appears on your phone, you confirm with your PIN, and your bundle is delivered within seconds.

⚙️ How STK Push Works

The process is designed to simplify M-PESA payments and reduce typing errors:

  1. A customer selects a product or service (e.g., a bundle on Bingwa Sokoni)
  2. The business system requests payment via Safaricom's API
  3. Safaricom sends an STK prompt to the customer's phone
  4. The customer enters their M-PESA PIN
  5. Payment confirmation is processed instantly
  6. The business delivers the product or service

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – STK Meaning & Push

❓ What does STK mean in M-PESA?

STK stands for Sim Toolkit. In M-PESA, STK Push is a payment method where a prompt is sent directly to your phone. You simply enter your PIN to confirm the payment.

❓ What is STK Push in M-PESA?

STK Push is a Safaricom M-PESA payment method where a payment prompt is automatically sent directly to your phone. You simply enter your PIN to complete the transaction – no need to type paybill or till numbers manually.

❓ Is STK Push safe to use?

Yes. STK Push is secure because you never share your M-PESA PIN with the business. The prompt comes directly from Safaricom, and you enter your PIN on your own device.

❓ Why did I not receive an STK Push prompt?

Common reasons include poor network signal, incorrect phone number entry, or temporary Safaricom system delays. Try again after a few minutes or restart your phone.

❓ Does STK Push work with Okoa Jahazi?

Yes, STK Push transactions work even if you have Okoa debt, as long as your available M-PESA balance (or Fuliza limit) can cover the payment. However, official USSD bundle purchases may still be blocked.

❓ How does Bingwa Sokoni use STK Push for bundles?

❓ What is the difference between STK Push and USSD?

USSD (like *544#) requires you to type codes and navigate menus. STK Push sends a prompt directly to your phone – you just enter your PIN and confirm. STK Push is faster and less error-prone.
