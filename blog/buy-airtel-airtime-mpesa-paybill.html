Method 1: Official Airtel Paybill (888777)

Airtel has an official Paybill number 888777 that accepts M‑PESA payments for airtime and bundles. Steps:

  1. Open M‑PESA → Lipa na M‑PESA → Paybill.
  2. Enter Business Number: 888777.
  3. Account Number: Your Airtel phone number (e.g., 0712345678).
  4. Enter Amount (e.g., 100 for Ksh 100 airtime).
  5. Confirm with M‑PESA PIN.

The airtime is usually credited within a minute. However, this method charges the official Airtel price, and it does not work if you have an active Airtel Okoa (Pewa) debt – the transaction may fail or be used to repay the loan.

For more on Airtel Okoa, see Airtel Okoa (Pewa) – How to Borrow and Still Buy Bundles.

Method 2: Reseller Buy Goods Till (e.g., 1621614)

Reseller platforms (like Bingwa Sokoni) offer Airtel bundles via a Buy Goods till. This method often gives you discounted prices and works even with outstanding Okoa debt.

  1. Open M‑PESA → Lipa na M‑PESA → Buy Goods.
  2. Enter Till Number: 1621614 (or another reseller till).
  3. Enter the exact amount for the bundle (e.g., 53 for 2GB).
  4. Confirm with PIN – the bundle is delivered instantly to your Airtel line.

Which Method Should You Use?

  • Use Paybill 888777 if: you prefer official channels, don’t mind paying full price, and have no Okoa/Pewa debt.
  • Use a reseller till (1621614) if: you want to save money, need to buy bundles even with debt, or want a quicker process (no account number required).

How to Check Your Airtel Balance After Purchase

After buying, you can check your Airtel balance by dialling *544# and selecting “Check Balance”. For data bundles, dial *544# → “Data” → “Check Balance”.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Airtel via M-PESA

❓ Can I buy Airtel bundles for another number using M-PESA?

Yes – both Paybill and reseller till methods allow you to specify a different recipient number. For Paybill, enter the recipient’s Airtel number as the account. For the till, some resellers have a gifting feature on their website where you enter the recipient’s number.

❓ Is it safe to use a reseller till?

Yes, as long as the till number is verified (e.g., 1621614 has a good reputation). M‑PESA STK push is secure – you never share your PIN with the reseller.

❓ Why does my Paybill transaction fail when I have Okoa debt?

Airtel’s system prioritises loan repayment. The payment is redirected to clear your debt instead of giving you new airtime. A reseller till bypasses this because it’s a “buy goods” transaction, not a direct airtime top‑up.

❓ Are there any extra M-PESA fees for these transactions?

No, both Paybill and Buy Goods transactions are free of M‑PESA charges.
