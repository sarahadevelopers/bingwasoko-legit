This guide covers all the official ways to turn your airtime into cash, the fees involved, and some important limits you should know.

⚠️ Important: Safaricom does not offer a direct SMS code for airtime conversion. The only official ways are via USSD (*100#) and the MySafaricom App. Avoid third‑party services that claim to convert via SMS – they are often scams.

🤔 What Does "Airtime to Cash" Mean?

Converting airtime to M‑PESA cash allows you to transfer the value of your Safaricom airtime balance into your M‑PESA wallet. Once converted, the money can be used for any M‑PESA transaction – sending to others, paying bills, buying goods, or withdrawing cash. It is a convenient way to unlock the value of airtime that you might not need for calls or data.

Safaricom charges a 10% service fee on the conversion amount. For example, if you convert Ksh 100, you receive Ksh 90 in your M‑PESA wallet, and Ksh 10 is retained as the conversion fee.

📞 Official Methods: USSD *100# and MySafaricom App

These are the only two legitimate methods offered by Safaricom:

  • USSD *100# – Quick and works on any phone, even without data.
  • MySafaricom App – Available for Android and iOS; includes a user‑friendly interface.

Both methods follow the same process and have the same fee structure.

📋 Step‑by‑Step via USSD *100#

  1. Dial *100# from your Safaricom line.
  2. Select "My Account" from the main menu.
  3. Choose "Convert Airtime to M‑PESA" (you may need to scroll).
  4. Enter the amount of airtime you wish to convert (minimum is Ksh 20, maximum varies – see limits below).
  5. Confirm the conversion – the system will show you the amount you will receive (after the 10% fee).
  6. You will receive a confirmation SMS from Safaricom, and the cash will be credited to your M‑PESA wallet instantly.

That is it – the whole process takes less than a minute.

📌 Tip: If you do not see the "Convert Airtime to M‑PESA" option, make sure your SIM is registered and active. The option is available to all prepaid and postpaid customers.

📱 Step‑by‑Step via MySafaricom App

  1. Open the MySafaricom App and log in with your PIN or biometrics.
  2. From the dashboard, navigate to "Airtime & Bundles".
  3. Select "Convert Airtime to M‑PESA".
  4. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  5. Review the fee and net amount, then confirm.
  6. The cash is sent to your M‑PESA wallet instantly – you will receive a push notification and an SMS.

The app gives you a clear breakdown of the fee and the final amount, making it easier to see exactly what you will get.

💰 Fees, Limits & Important Rules

  • Service Fee: 10% of the converted amount. E.g., convert Ksh 190, receive Ksh 450.
  • Minimum conversion: Ksh 20 (you may receive Ksh 18 after fee).
  • Maximum conversion per day: Usually Ksh 5,000 (this may vary depending on your account status).
  • Daily limit: You can convert up to the daily M‑PESA transaction limit, but the airtime conversion has its own cap of about Ksh 5,000 per day.
  • Eligibility: Only airtime purchased via official channels (scratch cards, M‑PESA top‑up, bank transfers) can be converted. Some promotional or bonus airtime may not be eligible.
  • Postpaid customers: You can convert airtime if you have a positive balance, but the process may differ slightly – check with Safaricom.

If you need to convert larger amounts or have issues, you can contact Safaricom customer care via *100# or call 100.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Airtime to M‑PESA Cash

❓ Is there an SMS code to convert Safaricom airtime to M‑PESA cash?

No – Safaricom does not have a dedicated SMS shortcode for airtime conversion. The official method is via USSD *100# or the MySafaricom App. However, you can initiate the conversion by dialling *100# and following the prompts – it is just as quick as sending an SMS.

❓ How do I convert airtime to M‑PESA cash using USSD?

Dial *100# → Select 'My Account' → 'Convert Airtime to M‑PESA' → Enter the amount you want to convert → Confirm. The cash will be sent to your M‑PESA wallet instantly, minus a 10% service fee.

❓ How much does Safaricom charge to convert airtime to cash?

Safaricom charges a 10% service fee on the amount you convert. For example, if you convert Ksh 100, you will receive Ksh 90 in your M‑PESA wallet, and Ksh 10 is deducted as the conversion fee.

❓ Can I convert airtime to M‑PESA cash via the MySafaricom App?

Yes – open the MySafaricom App, go to 'Airtime & Bundles', then select 'Convert Airtime to M‑PESA' and follow the on‑screen instructions. The process is similar to the USSD method.

❓ Are there any unofficial ways to sell airtime for cash in Kenya?

Yes – you can sell airtime to resellers or through online platforms, but be cautious of scams. The official Safaricom method is the safest and most reliable. Resellers may offer different rates, but they carry higher risk.

❓ What happens if I try to convert promotional or bonus airtime?

Promotional airtime (e.g., from "Tunukiwa" or "Happy Hours") may not be eligible for conversion. Only airtime purchased with real money can be converted. If the option is not available, the airtime may be promotional.

❓ Can I convert airtime if I have Okoa debt?

Yes – you can convert airtime to M‑PESA cash even if you have Okoa Jahazi debt, as long as you have sufficient airtime balance. The conversion does not affect your Okoa loan – you will still need to repay that separately.
