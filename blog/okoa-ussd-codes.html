📌 What is Okoa Jahazi?

Okoa Jahazi is Safaricom's credit facility that allows you to borrow airtime when you run out of credit. Eligible subscribers can borrow Ksh 10, 20, 50, or 100, which is automatically deducted from your next airtime top‑up. It is a helpful service for emergencies, but it is important to track your balance to avoid service restrictions.

📌 Quick fact: You must repay Okoa within 7 days. If you don't, your line may be barred from making outgoing calls or using data until the debt is cleared.

📱 USSD to Check Okoa Balance (Safaricom)

There are two main USSD methods to check your Okoa Jahazi balance:

  • Method 1 – *100#
    Dial *100# → Select My Account → Choose Okoa Jahazi → Your balance will be displayed.
  • Method 2 – *544#
    Dial *544# → Navigate to My Account or Okoa Jahazi → Check balance.

Both methods will show your outstanding loan amount and the repayment deadline.

⚠️ Note: If you have no active Okoa loan, you will see a message confirming that you have no outstanding balance.

💰 USSD to Borrow Okoa Airtime

To borrow airtime via USSD:

  1. Dial *100#.
  2. Select Okoa Jahazi or Borrow Airtime.
  3. Choose the amount you wish to borrow (Ksh 10, 20, 50, or 100 – depending on your eligibility).
  4. Confirm the loan – the airtime is added to your line immediately.

You will receive an SMS confirming the loan amount and repayment terms.

💡 Eligibility tip: Borrowing limits increase with consistent usage and timely repayment.

🔄 How to Repay Okoa via USSD

Repayment is automatic – when you top up airtime (via M‑PESA, scratch card, or bank transfer), the system deducts the outstanding Okoa amount first, and the remaining balance is added to your line. There is no separate USSD code for repayment, but you can check your outstanding balance using the methods above to know how much will be deducted.

If you want to repay early, simply top up any amount – the system will deduct the loan instantly.

📡 Airtel Okoa (Pewa) USSD Codes

Airtel Kenya's version of Okoa is called Pewa or Airtel Okoa. Here are the USSD codes:

  • Check Balance: Dial *544# → Select My Account → Airtel Okoa → View balance.
  • Borrow Airtime: Dial *544# → Choose Airtel Okoa → Select the amount (Ksh 10/20/50) → Confirm.
  • Repay: Automatic upon next recharge, similar to Safaricom.

You can also use the MyAirtel app for the same functions.
